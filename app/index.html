City of Stonks

Enter an address, watch a city rise.

A city rises out of how its stonks actually trade — the more it holds and moves, the bigger it gets and the more it unlocks.

This is an independent fan-made replica of cityofstonks.com/app, not officially affiliated and not the real generator. Nothing here is sent anywhere — an address is hashed in your browser into deterministic demo traits, and every building is drawn by this page's own procedural renderer, not the real site's artwork. The trait thresholds were reverse-engineered from the real site's own public demo data (see app/data/ in this repo) and are close, not exact.
